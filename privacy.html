Privacy Policy

Cronos Metrics · Effective 15 October 2026 · Operated by Cronos Metrics Dashboard

1. What Cronos Metrics is

Cronos Metrics ("the App") is an internal analytics tool operated by Cronos Metrics Dashboard ("we", "us"). It connects to social media accounts that we own and operate (on platforms such as TikTok and Instagram) to retrieve performance statistics for content we have published, and stores those statistics in a private reporting spreadsheet used by our marketing team.

2. Whose data is involved

The App only accesses accounts that we own and that an authorised member of our team has explicitly connected. It does not request, collect, or store personal data about other users of those platforms. Where a platform returns aggregated audience statistics (for example, the share of viewers by country), these are provided by the platform in aggregated form and cannot identify any individual.

3. Data we access

  • Basic profile information for our own connected accounts (account identifier, display name, username).
  • A list of content we have published (post or video identifiers, publish time, caption, link).
  • Performance statistics for that content (views, likes, comments, shares, and similar counts).

We do not access private messages, follower lists, or content belonging to other accounts.

4. How we use it

To measure the performance of our own content over time and report on it internally. The data is not used for advertising to individuals, sold, or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

5. Where it is stored and who can see it

Statistics are stored in a Google Sheet and reporting tools controlled by us, with access limited to authorised team members. Access tokens issued by the platforms are stored in a restricted configuration store and are never published.

6. Retention

We keep historical performance statistics so that trends can be compared over time. Access tokens are refreshed or revoked when no longer needed. You can revoke the App's access to an account at any time from the platform's own settings (for example, TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites), after which the App can no longer retrieve new data for that account.

7. Third-party platforms

Data is retrieved through the platforms' official developer interfaces, subject to their terms (including the TikTok Developer Terms and the Meta Platform Terms). Each platform's own privacy policy governs its handling of your data.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do. Questions about this policy can be sent to growth@cronos.com.
